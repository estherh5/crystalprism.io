What the Crystal Prism apps keep, and why

The apps at crystalprism.io are small, private tools built and run by one person, Esther Hoffman. They have no ads, and they sell or share nothing with anyone.

When you sign in with Google, an app keeps your name, email address and profile picture. It uses them to check your invitation and to show who you are signed in as. It asks Google for nothing else.

What you add to an app is stored so the app can show it back to you and to the people you share it with. Records are stored on Turso, and uploaded files on Cloudflare.

Visits are counted with Plausible, which sets no cookies and records no personal data. When a page breaks, a report of the error and the page it happened on is sent so it can be fixed.

To have your account and everything tied to it deleted, or to ask anything about this policy, email hoffman.esther@gmail.com.
